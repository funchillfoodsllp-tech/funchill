Our Journey

From Farm to Global Tables

Funchill Foods was founded with a single conviction — to transform Gujarat's locally grown potatoes into world-class frozen French fries and potato products that compete on quality with the world's best export brands.

Today, our products reach importers, QSR chains, and retail distributors across 25+ countries. Every buyer who sources from us gets the same product quality they approved in the sample — in every container, every time.

Farm-Sourced. IQF-Processed. Globally Trusted.
